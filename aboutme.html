TECHNICAL DESIGNER

I've always firmly believed that gaming is the most powerful medium for telling stories and moving people, creating unforgettable experiences that evoke deep emotions. This is what I strive to do whenever I design a game or a mechanic!

I'm Sam, a junior technical designer who thrives on creating awesome experiences. Playtesting and the iterative method are my ethos. I have experience in multiple areas of game design, a broad track record of student projects, and a six-month internship at a small indie studio under my belt. I bring passion, lighthearted energy, and a burning earnestness for making fun experiences.
